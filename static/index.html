Backtester

Same engine as the paper trader. Zones at any moment are built only from 4h candles that had closed by then; entries fill at the next candle's open; Bybit taker fees and funding are charged on every trade.

Optimizer

Searches entry settings and small changes to each coin's zone ladder, one search per coin. Ranked by net R after fees on the first 70 % of the period, then checked on the last 30 %, which it never saw. Prefer rows that hold up on the test side.
